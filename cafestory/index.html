Our Story

A café built around
the quiet hour.

CafeStory began in 2019 as a single roasting bench in Surat's Rander lane. Today it's a full house of coffee, wood-fired kitchen and community — still guided by the same idea: good things take time, and deserve yours.

Every bean is sourced direct, roasted in small batches, and served in a room designed to slow you down.
