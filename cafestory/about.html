Our Story

Six years, one
unhurried idea.

CafeStory was never meant to be fast. It was built to be the opposite — a room where coffee is roasted slowly, food is cooked over open flame, and time is allowed to pass the way it should.

01

A single bench in Rander

In 2019, our founder Meera Trivedi set up a roasting bench in a rented corner of a Rander lane — one drum roaster, twelve beans on the menu, four tables. There was no plan to expand; only a conviction that Surat deserved coffee treated with the same seriousness as the city's food.

Word travelled the way it does in old neighbourhoods: slowly, and by people who meant it.

02

The kitchen joins the story

By 2021, guests were staying past their coffee. A wood-fired oven arrived, and with it, a small kitchen team obsessed with 72-hour fermented dough, hand-rolled pasta and produce sourced within a day's drive of Surat.

The menu grew — but the pace never did. Everything is still made to order, in full view of the room.

03

Today, and what stays true

CafeStory now sources from 42 origins, roasts in small batches twice a week, and seats guests who return, sometimes daily, for the same window table. We're still the same size we chose to be — because the room, not the queue, was always the point.

What We Hold To

Three quiet rules

I.

Source Honestly

Every bean is traceable to a farm, every vegetable to a grower we've actually met. No shortcuts, no unnamed suppliers.

II.

Make It Slow

Dough ferments for 72 hours. Coffee is roasted in batches small enough to taste each one. Nothing here is rushed to save time.

III.

Keep the Room Warm

No table is turned before a guest is ready to leave. Hospitality, to us, means never making someone feel like the clock is running.
